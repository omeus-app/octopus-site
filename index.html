The story

The Buffet Problem

We are living in an era of intellectual diabetes. We consume fragments, bullet points, and AI summaries that make us feel smart while our comprehension engine atrophies.

The 10-Kilometer Rule

If your only goal is to get to a destination 10 kilometers away, you take a taxi. But if your goal is to strengthen your legs, you have to walk. There is no other way.

octopus is designed for the walk. We make that walk as engaging and immersive as possible — but we refuse to take the walk for you.

The Cheapest Gym Membership

We are building the cheapest gym membership for the most sophisticated muscle in the body. And unlike every other gym, our goal isn't to keep you coming back. It's to make you strong enough to walk away.
